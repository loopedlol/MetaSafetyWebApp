## Screenshot provenance

Captured on 2026-09-19 from source commit `4c0934f140c9c6d2b71da5120c6dd5c5df58b83c`, with the unchanged Vite/Express app and an isolated temporary SQLite database. The account, site inputs, and workflow records were fictional local demonstration data. The app's existing sample hazard content was used; no real workplace photos, user accounts, credentials, or saved production records were loaded.

The dashboard and glasses images were captured directly from Chromium. The glasses view uses the existing browser-preview adapter. It is not a hardware photograph or proof of real camera capture, voice input, AI interpretation, or deployment.
